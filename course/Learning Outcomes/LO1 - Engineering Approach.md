You apply an **engineering approach** to create software systems in **progressive steps**, while applying **system thinking** during **informed decision making**. 

### Engineering approach 

- You demonstrate measurable feedback cycles during your work, to validate and guide your decisions and technical solutions.
- You apply an engineering approach to aim at improving your software engineering skills.  

### Progressive steps 

- You demonstrate progress in taking small steps towards your goal. This could be done e.g. by evidence-based experiments with concepts relevant to your project, including the use of GenAI-assisted prototyping and validation, and decide, based on outcomes, whether an experiment might add value to the project.
