## What

Sustainable software development focuses on designing and building software that minimises energy consumption and resource usage throughout its lifecycle. This includes efficient code, optimised algorithms, scalable architectures, and conscious use of cloud and data resources (Green Software Foundation, 2023). 

## Why

Software has a direct impact on energy use in data centres, cloud platforms, and end-user devices. Sustainable software development supports the United Nations [Sustainable Development Goals](https://sdgs.un.org/goals), particularly SDG 9 (Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure), SDG 12 (Responsible Consumption and Production), and SDG 13 (Climate Action). By developing energy-efficient software, developers contribute to reducing carbon emissions and promoting responsible digital innovation (United Nations, 2015; OECD, 2022). 

## How

There are multiple ways of showing you considered 'sustainability' for your project. Mainly in your role as a software designer and advisor, you can contribute to sustainability and energy-efficient software. For example, by writing efficient and maintainable code, reducing unnecessary data processing, and choosing architectures such as serverless or microservices when appropriate.  

Choose one or more of these approaches: 

1. Analyse: integrate sustainability requirements as non-functional requirements. 
2. Design: justify architectural choices from a sustainability perspective. 
3. Implement: show sustainable practices. 
4. Manage & control: measure software energy consumption and optimise cloud usage.
5. Advice: reflect on the impact and suggest possible improvements.
