The assessment process that we follow in this topic is based on the [assessment policy](https://beleidswiki.fhict.nl/doku.php?id=en:beleid:toetsbeleid "Link") of Fontys ICT.

Here we summarize the process and the four assessment moments.

As a student you build up a portfolio with validated content. This means that work you include in your portfolio has been at least offered once for feedback. You got feedback that it is suitable content for your portfolio and probably also had some feedback where improvements or further development are possible. Therefore, you should aim at regularly feedback from your teachers on your work. Keep in mind, it doesn't have to be 'done'. Early feedback will help getting early insight in your work and process. It also gives the coaches the opportunity to give adequate and timely feedback, and it also ensures authenticity and balanced learning curve over time. Students are responsible to also look for relevant stakeholders and experts, besides their coaches, to validate their work. This is to guarantee feedback triangulation.

There are four moments in the semester to hand-in a portfolio reviews.  
  
Three of these moments have the goal to _show & grow_. Your teacherteam will give their collecrtive view on how you are progressing on the learning outcomes of this semester. Also here, the work in your portfolio doesn't have to be done. 

After submission of your portfolio the teacherteam will plan a moment to communicate their assessment result with you. This moment is within 1-2 weeks after the deadline mentioned in Canvas. This gives the team time to look at your portfolio, find a moment to align with the other teachers and formulate their collective view on your work. The deadlines for submission are planned in week 4-6, 10-12 and 16-17. At the start of the semester, the coaches will communicate the exact deadlines to hand-in a snapshot of the portfolio to be assessed.

The fourth portfolio submission is your final work. This is the last chance to rework the feedback of the last assessment and update your portfolio with final work of the last weeks. Based with the input of this portfolio, feedpulse, and your study attitude is input for the teacher team to determine the final semester grade.
