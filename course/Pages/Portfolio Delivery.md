As described in the [semester assessment process](/courses/15686/pages/semester-assessment-process "Semester Assessment Process"), there are three formal moments in the semester when you receive feedback on your progress using the development-oriented feedback scale (learning outcomes). The format of this feedback is determined by your teaching team and will be communicated at least two weeks before each assessment.

The fourth and final delivery is your portfolio, which serves as the basis for your teachers to determine your semester grade during the final assessor meeting.

You will build your portfolio in the [Portflow](/accounts/1/external_tools/23360?launch_type=global_navigation "Link") tool in Canvas. Your portfolio should contain only validated work, meaning:

- All content has been discussed at least once with a teacher or expert.
- Feedback received is recorded in Feedpulse or is gathered through a 'feedback request' in Portflow.
- You clearly demonstrate how you’ve applied the feedback to your work or outline any remaining plans for implementation.

To guide your portfolio submissions, refer to the three core assignment descriptions to get some idea of what should be minimally included.
