During fall of 2025 we had a major redesign of the _complex software systems_ topic that is rolled out in the spring of 2026.  
We have moved away from a enterprise software focus to a broader focus on different complex software systems. In all development of complex software systems the commonality for a software professional is having a software engineering mindset which is lead by quality criteria that fit the context of the problem and are balanced with the resources that are available to the customer.   
  
Instead of technology specific learning outcomes we have now a set of three more general learning outcomes that should lead to:

*   Less prescriptive LOs in terms of technologies and expected deliverables
*   A Software Engineering Mindset in which applied research and progressive development are embedded
*   Offer space (in time & knowledge) for students to explore their personal interests by introducing _personal flavors._
*   Add elements necessary for complex software systems like system thinking, and making choices fitting environment & customer needs  

Furthermore changes have been made to the semester set-up to:

*   Have a more central role for the proftaak in learning & applying
*   Improved cooperation between students, teachers, and stakeholders by design
