## What

Setting goals means defining clear, achievable objectives for your study, projects, and future career. Taking action refers to the concrete steps you take to work towards these goals. Together, they form a structured approach to personal and professional development. 

## Why 

Without clear goals, it is easy to lose focus or motivation. Goal setting gives direction and helps you prioritise tasks. Taking action ensures that plans turn into progress.  

## How

1. Think about “Where do I see myself in 3-5 years?”. What role do I have? In what type of organisation do I work? What kind of projects am I working on?  
2. When you have an idea for the future think about “What knowledge and/or skills am I lacking?” These are your goals. Break large objectives into smaller, manageable tasks. Create SMART goals (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound).  
3. Create an action plan and planning system to track your progress. Which goals are you planning to achieve this semester? And which goals are for graduation? 
4. Regularly reflect on what works and adjust your approach when needed. 
5. Stay proactive: seek feedback, ask for support when required, and celebrate milestones.  
6. At the end of the semester: add a reflection. What were your goals? What did you accomplish? What are your next steps?
