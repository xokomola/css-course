## What

Now you're close to graduation, you need to decide (if you haven't already) what career in software you want to pursue. The life of a Software Engineer is not simple. You'll struggle with complexities in projects all day long. And then comes private life, partner, kids, friends and hobbies. It means all plans will have less chance of happening if you do not prepare for them. What can help you is to have a clear vision of what you want to achieve in your life and prioritize.   

## Why

The goal of this section is to help you prepare for future challenges. Even after your studies, you aren't done learning. How can you challenge yourself to keep learning for the rest of your career? How to keep track of all the new technologies and how to spot these career breaking trends? 

Besides keeping up with new trends, you want to invest in skills that do not change (that fast). What we mean by this, is investing in developing skills that are present in more domains or technologies, so you can reuse them between projects, programming languages, et cetera. 

Some examples: 

- Good UI design 
- Understanding of the domain of the customer 
- Advising and trend analysis 
- Teaching and organizing knowledge transfer in the team 
- Modelling and design 
- Debugging 
- Readability of code 
- Efficiency analysis and improvement 
- Communication protocols 
- Security standards 
- Code review skills 
- Documentation skills 
- Presenting skills 
- Problem-solving 
- Communication skills 
- Et cetera 

## How

- Trend watching: Many companies and experts specialize in predicting and measuring trends. Some of them like [Gartner](https://www.gartner.com/en/research/methodologies/gartner-hype-cycle) even get so influential that their models are de facto standard when talking about trends. They provide (paid) predictions at least once a year. 

Another (free) source of trends related wisdom is the [Thoughtworks Technology Radar](https://www.thoughtworks.com/radar). (An opinionated guide to technology frontiers). Look for interesting new technology in the Assess and Trial rings (and read the motivation why they are included in the tech radar). See what direction we might go in with tools and techniques and consider experimenting with one (or even use one in the group project if relevant). 

- Choose some general skills that you would like to develop for your future career. Make them part of your goals this semester.
