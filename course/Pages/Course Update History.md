### Changes Spring 2026  
During fall of 2025 we had a major redesign of the _complex software systems_ topic that is rolled out in the spring of 2026.

We have moved away from a enterprise software focus to a broader focus on different types of complex software systems.

A commonality with all complex software systems is that you need to approach them with a software engineering mindset, have measurable control over your software quality attributes, and create a solid software development environemnt, maintenance and operational processes. All these elements are tailored to  fit the context of the problem and should be balanced with the resources and organisation of your customer. 

These skills have been translated to three learning outcomes that act as anchors in complex environments, instead of technology specific learning outcomes. Which technologies you need will emerge from your research. Also there is room in the _personal flavors_ to learn about new technologies.

This new set of learning outcomes have led to:

- Less prescriptive LOs in terms of technologies and expected deliverables
    
- A Software Engineering Mindset in which applied research and progressive development are embedded
    
- There is now room for students to learn new technologies that fit their personal interests by introducing _personal flavors (personal experiments)._
- Added elements to deal with for complex software systems like system thinking, and making choices fitting environment & customer needs  
    

  
Furthermore changes have been made to the semester set-up to:

- Have a more central role for the group project to learn new concepts & apply them in a realistic setting
    
- Improved cooperation between students, teachers, and stakeholders by design
