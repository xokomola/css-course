## What

The process of growing in your intercultural competences (becoming more sensitive and aware of differences, knowing how do deal with them, etc.) is intercultural development. 

## Why

Intercultural competence is achieved slowly and with conscious effort. Without explicit effort and attention to it you can make little progress even if you interact and work with people from other cultures and travel abroad. Therefore, we give you space and time to work on your growth this semester.  

## How

This semester you work on your growth in intercultural competences. You can achieve this in several ways. Select one or more of the options below: 

### Option 1: Measure your current intercultural competence, reflect on it and work on your growth!

For this option, we make use of an external tool to measure your intercultural competence called [IDI](https://idiinventory.com/). Costs are covered by Fontys ICT. Follow these steps: 

- At the start of the semester send an e-mail to [fontysict-icd@fontys.nl](mailto:fontysict-icd@fontys.nl) with subject "IDI student", provide your name and e-mail if you prefer to use a non-fontys e-mail.  
- You will receive a link to a survey which you need to fill in (30 min) 
- You will receive a report with your score and a tutorial how to interpret it (plan 2 hours to  read your report and follow the tutorial). The tutorial is to be found in the main menu (second item on the left) 
- You will also receive an intercultural development plan for your level of competence. Use it throughout the semester to work on your development. Discuss your score and your development plan with your semester coach.  
- Use the plan and reflection on its implementation in your portfolio 

### Option 2: Improve your skills to work in multicultural teams

For this option, you need to be part of a multicultural team. You will work on improving your intercultural sensitivity and competence by detecting challenges in the group caused by cultural differences and then attempting to resolve these challenges.  

Follow these steps for this option: 

- Fill in this [short questionnaire.](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdAdeDlAjiYjlCUEwdf3lAl5s950jXXsTR5Ed1QhMo5JaI7tQ/viewform?usp=sharing&ouid=109516551726090176589) This questionnaire will help you with detecting the challenges in your group.   
- Reflect on the origin of the challenges using knowledge from semester 2 and 3 on communication style differences and the Hofstede dimensions. 
- Make a plan on how you want to address these challenges in the group. Discuss your plan with your Semester Coach. 
- Record all your findings so far in your portfolio. Provide evidence where possible by describing situations or experiences. 
- Later in the semester: Reflect on the success to address these challenges in your portfolio, provide evidence for this.  

### Option 3: Something else? 

If you have another idea on how to work on your intercultural development, discuss it with your semester coach and if it is approved you can go ahead. For example, you may want to work on your [Bonus edubadge Intercultural Competence](https://www.studyabroadfontysict.nl/) portfolio (see bottom of the linked page) if you have followed international weeks, exchanges abroad, internships abroad, etc.
