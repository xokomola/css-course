## What  

This module is a summary of concepts that we assume you understand and can apply. It gives you an overview of expected knowledge, with some direct links to refresh when necessary. In case you have succeeded in an earlier semester on the topic of full-stack development, you have acquired this knowledge. 

## Why 

In our curriculum, you can follow a flexible study path. This might result in not meeting the assumed skillset at the start of a semester. If you have not followed the topic ‘full-stack development’ in the past, we urge you to self-study the concepts mentioned below. Also, if you did pass the ‘full-stack development’ semester, do not feel fully comfortable with some of the concepts. Take some time to refresh those skills.  

## How 

If you want to refresh some knowledge, take the time in week 1 of the semester to determine which concepts need refreshment and self-study them. Discuss your plans with your teachers. 

If you did not follow Full-stack Development, the advice is to self-study all the concepts in week 19-20 of the previous semester. You can continue this self-study in week 1.  

Plan in week 19 and discuss this with one of the teachers of complex software systems. Practicing is best accomplished in a small personal project in which you investigate and apply technical skills. To support your development, we expect you to design a simple software development process which is partially automated by a CI-CD pipeline. 

By the end of the first week of this semester you have a talk with any of the teachers about the environment and project you have set up. You explain in this talk your decisions. We also have a talk about the meaning of software quality and what are ways to demonstrate this intended quality in your software projects. 

## Assumed knowledge

- You can explain how to develop software in an Agile way and make clear how your stakeholders have a central role in this process. 
- You can explain what a software process is and are able to design and realize a simple process supporting your personal demo project. 
- You can select the appropriate tools to partially automate a part of your software process 
- You can explain what Software Quality is. You can identify important quality criteria and identify and use tools that can quantify these criteria.
