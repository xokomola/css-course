## What

Ethics is defined as: “moral principles that govern a person's behaviour or the conducting of an activity.” ([Oxford Languages, 2026](https://languages.oup.com/google-dictionary-en/)). In your job as a software engineer, you will often need to think about the ethics of what you are creating. Besides the ethical part of engineering, you will also need to deal with laws and regulations. 

## Why

- Your software will have impact on a (large) number of people. Therefore, you need to think about the effect your software can have. Think about how social media changed how we interact with each other. 
- Furthermore, every person has his own biases. These will influence the applications you create. 
- Besides this, a lot of the time you will work in a project team, and your behaviour will influence the team dynamics. Think about changing code without communication.  
- At last, as an engineer, you will have to deal with legislation and regulations, think about ISO certificates, AVG, GDPR, etc. 

## How 

1. Make an ethical analysis, for example with the [Technology Impact Cycle Tool (TICT)](http://TICT.io), the [tarot cards of tech](https://tarotcardsoftech.artefactgroup.com/), the expert question cards (ISSD), etc. 
2. You can look into the [courses](https://www.tict.io/courses) from TICT for a deeper understanding of (one of) the ten topics. 
3. Make it a part of your research, take a look at the methods within the DOT framework (Human Impact Score, Silicon Sampling, Ethical check). 
4. Discuss within the group and with your stakeholders. 
5. Modify your design/project based on your findings.
