## What

To get to know how you function and perform in your learning, related to the learning outcomes, you need to collect feedback. Purposely gather this information by asking specific questions to students, teachers, and professionals in the field of work. Translate the feedback into concrete actions, so you can improve the quality of your work and take ownership of your learning process. 

## Why 

It is essential to regularly request your teachers' feedback on your work, planning, approach, and progress. Doing this gives clear guidance towards achieving the learning outcomes. Furthermore, it gives your teacher a means to help you improve your learning process. 

## How

You frequently plan a feedback session with your teacher. There, you demonstrate your work, discuss your approach, and chosen solutions. You can ask feedback on your project as well as on your experiments and soft skills. Report the answers formulated based on your teachers' feedback as soon as possible at the latest checkpoint in FeedPulse. Next up, notify your teacher and ask to grant a smiley rating. 

Besides asking for feedback from your teachers, ask for feedback from fellow team members, the rest of the CSS community (fellow students, other teachers), external experts (teachers from different courses, professionals in the field of work), the company you are doing your project for, et cetera. Document this feedback as well.  

We do not only expect you to ask for feedback but give feedback to your fellow students as well. Consider using Feedpulse peer feedback to give and receive feedback from and to your team members. 

In your PDP and/or portfolio, you should reflect on received feedback. Show what you have done with the feedback and how it inspired improvement. Also discuss this with your teachers.
