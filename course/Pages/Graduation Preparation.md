## What 

If this is your first advanced semester,  your next semester will be: 

- an advanced semester 
- free study area (minor)
- Considering a minor abroad? More information can be found here: https://www.studyabroadfontysict.nl/

If this is your second advanced semester, your next semester will be:  

- Graduation 

You can find all the information for next semester via [Study Guide Fontys ICT - Home](https://studyguide.fontysict.nl/) 
Discuss your options with your semestercoach.  

## Why

Learning outcome **Personal leadership**: 

> You demonstrate leadership in your own development and learning ability as a long-term IT professional and reflect critically on this. You take sustainable action to achieve your goals, or make adjustments where necessary.

The main question here is "**What kind of IT professional do I want to be?**" 
An answer to this question helps you in finding a graduation project that suits you.

## How

Advanced semester or free study area 

1. Reflect on your development as an IT professional and think about what your goal is as an IT professional.  
2. Broad exploration: 
	1. Research career perspectives  
	2. Explore your options [Study Guide Fontys ICT - Home](https://studyguide.fontysict.nl) and create a shortlist of topics you are interested in.  
3. Exploration in depth: gather more information on the topics you are interested in. Join events, ask fellow students and teachers.  
4. Join *graduation tables* to hear experiences from first hand and explore graduation possibilities. At these tables you meet our current *graduation students* and discover what they are working.

### Graduation 

Enroll in the canvas course [Graduation Preparation](TODO) (link to be added). 
In this course you will find four activities:  

1. **Professional focus**. Define your professional profile, reflect on your competencies and update your resume/portfolio.  
2. **Graduation table**. Explore the graduation table themes, connect with teachers and join a learning community that fits your ambition.  
3. **Graduation context**. Find a suitable graduation position at a company, research group, start-up, or abroad and make contact.  
4. **Project proposal**. Define your assignment, write your project proposal and submit it for approval.
   
### Role of the semestercoach 

Your semestercoach will: 

- inform you about your options;
- provide feedback on your activities and preparation;
- challenge you and be critical about how you come to your choices regarding your career path, your ways and skills when pursuing opportunities;
- help you to know how and where to develop the required skills.
