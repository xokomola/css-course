## What

Personal Flavors are those subjects that you choose to study during this semester in addition to the core skills that we expect to be demonstrated by every student. 

You choose one or more personal flavors that fit your personal taste and will investigate these subject(s) as personal experiments during the semester worked out in one or more individual projects. You make sure that the individual project has also some clear foundations on the core skills you study during the semester. On top of that, your personal flavor skills are added. It would be a nice bonus if the insights of these experiments could also be a driver for the group projects. 

We will provide you with some domains that serve as a source of inspiration. You are free to explore also other domains. If you make sure to match the condition that your domain is related to software engineering and gives you the possibility to do your work related to the three learning outcomes in which your core skills are positioned:  software engineering, software quality, and software maintenance.  

## Why

The personal flavor projects are there for a few reasons, it offers: 

- The possibility to explore those subjects related to software engineering, which you are interested in. 
- A demonstration of your personal skills and abilities in another context/problem setting. 
- An environment in which you further develop your software engineering, software quality, and software maintenance skills within a very specific domain of your own choice.   

## How

Within this context, you work out at least two experiments following an applied research methodology (hint: DOT-framework).  Your activities will aim at gaining knowledge to solve a specific question, applying it, and validating the end results. These new insights and activities should contribute to your learning outcomes: software engineering, software quality, and software maintenance. Be aware that contributions can be more than a working prototype. Within your experiments you can also work out advices on how your insights can possibly contribute to maintenance and other quality aspects.

Tip: Timebox and plan ahead these experiments. We aim at fast prototyping, so these personal flavor experiments prefarabily have a run through of one sprint. By the end of a sprint you can decide if you want to iterate further for one sprint, or take up another personal flavor experiment. 

Note: Although a hint is given to the DOT framework, we do not expect your time to go into large theoretical reports. You should demonstrate that you can set up and perform experiments by fast prototyping your new ideas while following a methodological approach.  This is another demonstration of your engineering mindset.
